Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Mochi Guard is a focus app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your focus history and Screen Time stay on your iPhone, we have no accounts or servers that store your data, and we don't show ads or track you.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Your Mochi data. Your cat's name, cookies, yarn, room, focus history, streak, goals and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive them.
  • The apps you choose to guard. You pick apps in Apple's Screen Time picker. Apple gives Mochi Guard only private tokens, not the names of the apps, and the tokens stay on your device.
  • Screen Time reports. The hours shown in the app (for example "You scrolled" and "Your most used apps") are drawn by iOS on your device. Mochi Guard cannot read or send these numbers.
  • Notifications. Reminders (for example when a focus ends or for your streak) are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Mochi Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether Pro is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your App Store purchase records, and basic technical information such as device model, iOS version, country and IP address. It does not receive your name or email, or anything about your Screen Time.

What we don't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, and no selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No analytics tools inside the app.
  • Data from Apple's Screen Time tools is used only to guard your apps on your own device, as Apple requires.

Children

Mochi Guard is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children. Since your Mochi data stays on your device, we don't hold personal information about you or your family.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all Mochi data from your iPhone. To ask RevenueCat to delete the purchase records linked to your anonymous ID, email us and we will forward the request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
