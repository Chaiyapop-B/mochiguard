Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for Mochi? Write to mochiguard.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Why does Mochi Guard need Screen Time access?

Apple's Screen Time tools are the only way an app can put a shield over other apps. You choose the apps in Apple's own picker, and Apple never tells Mochi Guard which apps they are.

How do I change the apps Mochi guards?

Tap the basket in Mochi's room, then Edit. Depending on how strict you set Mochi, changes may start after a short wait.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Mochi Guard. Apple handles all subscriptions.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Tap the sign in Mochi's room, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I turn Mochi Guard off?

Delete the app, or turn off its Screen Time access in Settings, Screen Time. Your guarded apps open normally again.
